# Data retention decisions

Prepared 2026-10-28; finalized 2026-10-29, Asia/Calcutta. Status: **DECISION REQUIRED — product or operational policy is undefined.** No legal retention period is asserted. Automated business-record deletion remains disabled; this task neither deletes historical evidence nor adds cleanup schedules. Existing object replacement and ephemeral socket-transport cleanup retain their established behavior.

Read the detailed [retention inventory](PORISROM-REMAINING-PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS.md#retention-inventory). “Owner” below means an accountable role to assign, not an invented staff member or a claim about legal ownership. Product/privacy owners must obtain appropriate legal advice for actual jurisdictions and purposes; no compliance certification is made here.

For each category choose a purpose-bound retention trigger and period, hold exceptions, access roles, deletion/anonymization method, backup expiry treatment and accountable operator. Record approvals/date before enabling automation. The options below are alternatives without invented periods.

## Explicit approval questions

- Which responsible role owns each row, and who may approve/export/erase data?
- Which retention trigger and period apply to each purpose and deployment jurisdiction? Which records require legal, dispute, fraud or security holds?
- For identity/company files, may bytes be deleted after review while retaining the decision? How are rejected/replaced assets and failed cleanup reconciled?
- What verified process authorizes an erasure request, and what information may be anonymized while preserving financial/moderation integrity?
- How are immutable backups, object versions, replicas, exports and restored datasets included in expiry/erasure obligations? Who verifies completion?
- What are the audit immutability/export requirements and access-review frequency?
- What evidence proves a hold was applied/released and that cleanup cannot delete unrelated objects or reactivate blocked relationships?

No default answers are silently selected. These decisions do not block isolated synthetic staging, provided its data never becomes a real-user environment. They block production acceptance for real personal/financial data. After approval, deletion needs a separately reviewed implementation, dry-run inventory, authorization and restore/dependency regression tests; changing this document alone must not enable deletion.
